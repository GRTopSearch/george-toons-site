Youtube Animations Tool

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Youtube Animations Tool is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the George Toons YouTube channel. This policy explains how it handles data.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the tool accesses

  • Channel details, such as the channel name, subscriber count and branding.
  • Video details, such as titles, descriptions, privacy settings and view counts.
  • Channel analytics, such as views, watch time and subscribers gained or lost. These are totals, and the tool doesn't identify individual viewers.

How data is used

The data is used only to show reports to the channel owner, upload the channel's videos, and update the channel's settings when the owner asks.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens and saved reports are stored on the channel owner's own computer. The tool doesn't have a server, and it doesn't sell data or use it for advertising. The channel owner may use AI assistants, including Anthropic's Claude, to run the tool and read its reports, so channel data can be processed by those services at the owner's direction.

Cookies

The tool doesn't place cookies or other tracking technology on any device. This website doesn't use them either, though the service hosting it may keep standard server logs.

Removing access and data

Access can be removed at any time from the Google security settings page. Saved reports and sign-in tokens are removed by deleting them from the owner's computer.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
